Young-Chae Son

Welcome! I'm Youngchae Son, a robotics researcher focusing on intelligent robot control using Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) frameworks.

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Robotics at Dongguk University, Seoul, under the supervision of Prof. Soochul Lim at the Interactive Robotics Lab.

My research centers around integrating AI-based multimodal reasoning with real-world robotic systems to enable flexible and generalizable control. This site contains my research projects, publications, and academic profile.

Research

I'm interested in computer vision, vision-language models, and robotic planning. Most of my research is about inferring the physical world and thermal awareness for robotic decision-making.
